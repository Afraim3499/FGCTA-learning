# Lurnava / Elite Trading Academy

# Non-Duplicate 10-Level Master Syllabus

## Core + Forex + Gold + Crypto Roadways

---

# Global Card Architecture

Each normal module should contain:

* 15 Core concept cards
* 8–10 Forex specialized roadway cards
* 8–10 Gold specialized roadway cards
* 8–10 Crypto specialized roadway cards

Approximate cards per module: 39–45.

The roadways must not repeat the Core cards.
They must show how the same concept behaves differently inside Forex, Gold, and Crypto.

---

# Anti-Duplication Rule

Each module must pass this question:

**What new skill does the learner gain here that they did not have before?**

If the answer is weak, the module should be removed, merged, or rewritten.

---

# Level 0 — Market Reading Foundations

## Level Ownership

Level 0 owns observation, market reading, evidence, structure, condition, location, asset-specific reading, practice discipline, and note-taking.

It does not teach planning, execution, sizing, trade management, strategy construction, or certification.

## Strategy Lab Mode

Observation only.

---

## 0.1 — What Lurnava Teaches: Reading, Practice, Review
### New Learning
Learners understand the academy method and stop looking for signals.
### Core
Training loop, reading vs copying, practice vs random clicking, review as skill.
### Forex Roadway

### Gold Roadway

### Crypto Roadway
