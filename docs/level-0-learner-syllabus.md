# Level 0 Learner Syllabus

## Level 0 Purpose

Level 0 provides the foundational training program for market reading. It is designed to establish structured visual discipline before learners study execution systems or active trade setups.

* **Market Reading Foundations**: Level 0 teaches you how to observe price charts, identify structure, classify environmental conditions, and evaluate location quality.
* **No Signal Hype**: This level does not teach buy/sell setups, trading entries, or make any promises of profitability. We treat charts as historical transaction records, not magic crystal balls.
* **Process Over Prediction**: The curriculum focuses on building repeatable cognitive workflows—observing, classifying, verifying, practicing, and reviewing your decisions.
* **Preparation for Level 1**: Completing this training prepares you to enter Level 1, where you will study active order matching mechanics, order flow, and risk models.

## What Level 0 Does Not Teach Yet

To maintain educational integrity and prevent premature risk, Level 0 strictly excludes the following topics:

* **No Live Trading Promises**: We do not guarantee profits, show how to double accounts, or encourage premature live capital exposure.
* **No Trade Entry Systems**: You will not learn where to buy, where to sell, or where to put stop-losses in live conditions.
* **No Advanced Risk Models**: Position sizing, leverage calculations, and portfolio drawdown management are not taught here.
* **No Trade Management**: Managing active positions, trailing stops, or scaling out of trades are excluded.
* **Reason**: These concepts require a rock-solid ability to read market facts first. They are extensively covered in **Level 1** and beyond.

## Level 0 Completion Outcome

Upon successful graduation from Level 0, you will have built the following capabilities:

1. **Observe Market Facts**: Isolate observable coordinates (close levels, sweeps, wicks) from psychological assumptions.
2. **Deconstruct Candles**: Treat candlestick charts as transaction histories and identify high-quality candle close holds.
3. **Map Structure**: Identify swing highs/lows, trends, range boundaries, breaks, and structural failures.
4. **Grade Reading Clarity**: Audit evidence stacks and classify readings into Clear, Mixed, or Unclear states.
5. **Locate Key Edges**: Differentiate high-priority reaction boundaries from noisy, middle-range chop.
6. **Handle Asset Context**: Identify and filter out gold wicks, forex session transitions, and crypto venue/derivative traps.
7. **Formulate Verification**: Write specific, testable, yes/no falsifiable conditions that must occur to prove a reading.
8. **Practice with Process**: Run simulator sessions focused on process scores rather than virtual PnL, holding hands inactive when rules dictate.
9. **Document Pre-Action Logs**: Write useful practice notes capturing reasoning, invalidation, and mistake tags before outcomes occur.
